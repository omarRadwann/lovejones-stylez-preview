Meet the owner

Christina's story should be the first thing clients feel.

New York to Raleigh

24+ years in hair. 14 years licensed. Raleigh salon owner.

Christina Jones is originally from New York City and Upstate New York. She has worked in professional salons, shadowed salon owners, and opened Love Jones Stylez in the Raleigh area.

Her specialty areas are hair coloring techniques, haircutting techniques, natural hair styling, hair treatments, transformations, and understanding the science behind each client's hair needs.
