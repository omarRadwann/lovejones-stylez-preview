CROWN

Standing with the CROWN Act and natural hair freedom

A position, not a tagline

Your hair is not a barrier. It's the point.

The CROWN Act exists because Black hair has been graded as "unprofessional" in schools, in offices, in courtrooms. Love Jones Stylez is built on the opposite belief: that healthy natural hair, locz, texture, and culture belong at the center of the room — not on its edges.

— Christina Jones, owner

Meet the owner

The hands behind every healthy reset.

New York to Raleigh

24+ years in hair. 14 years licensed. Raleigh salon owner.

Christina Jones is originally from New York City and Upstate New York. She has worked in professional salons, shadowed salon owners, and opened Love Jones Stylez in the Raleigh area.

Her specialty areas are hair coloring techniques, haircutting techniques, natural hair styling, hair treatments, transformations, and understanding the science behind each client's hair needs.

"As soon as healing takes place, go out and heal someone else." - Maya Angelou

Before your visit

A few notes so the chair stays calm.

01

No children or extra people if they are not being serviced due to seating for other customers.

02

For hair color preparation, remove any style and untwist at the root for locs.

03

Take down services not previously provided by LJS may include an extra charge.

04

A $50 deposit is required for services over $150 to cover supplies needed for service.

05

Prices are non-negotiable.
